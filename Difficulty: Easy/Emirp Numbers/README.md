<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/emirp-numbers4337/1?page=9">Emirp Numbers</a></h2><h3>Difficulty Level : Difficulty: Easy</h3><hr><div class="problems_problem_content__Xm_eO" style="--text-color: var(--problem-text-color); --darkreader-text--text-color: var(--darkreader-text--problem-text-color); --darkreader-border--text-color: var(--darkreader-border--problem-text-color);"><p class="PDq2pG_selectionAnchorContainer" data-start="350" data-end="426">Given an integer n, find all Emirp numbers in the range <strong data-start="414" data-end="425">[13, n]</strong>.&nbsp;An Emirp number is a prime number whose decimal reverse is also prime, the reversed number is different from the original number, and the reversed number is not greater than n.</p>
<p data-start="616" data-end="678"><strong data-start="616" data-end="625">Note:</strong> Palindromic primes are not considered Emirp numbers.</p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Examples:</strong></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>n = 40
<strong>Output: </strong>13 31
<strong>Explanation: </strong>Only 13 and 31 are prime numbers which are satisfying the given conditions.</span>
</pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>n = 80
<strong>Output: </strong>13 17 31 37 71 73&nbsp;
<strong>Explanation: </strong>Only 13, 17, 31, 37, 71 and 73 are prime numbers which satisfy the given conditions.</span>
